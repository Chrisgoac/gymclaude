## Full description

GymLog is a strength training log for recording what you do at the gym, without distractions.
Note: the app is currently available in Spanish only.

FREE
• Create routines and follow them day by day; the app suggests the next one.
• Log every set (weight, reps, warm-up) with a rest timer.
• Browse your history and edit past workouts.
• See your progress: volume, personal records and per-exercise charts.
• Record your body weight and measurements, and keep progress photos.
• Unlock achievements.
• Use several gyms and custom exercises.
• Export and import a copy of your data.
• Train offline: everything is saved on your phone and syncs when you are back online, including with the web
  version.

GYMLOG PRO (monthly or annual subscription)
Pro adds AI-powered features:
• AI Coach: ask about your training and get guidance based on your data (week, plateaus, measurements).
• AI mesocycles: generate a multi-week plan that you review and save as routines.
• Intelligent progress report.
Everything else stays free. If you cancel, your data is still yours to view and export. The subscription renews
automatically until you cancel it in Google Play.

ABOUT THE AI
The Coach, mesocycles and reports are generated by artificial intelligence: they can be wrong, they are not
medical advice and they do not replace a coach or a health professional. If you feel pain or have health
concerns, see a professional. You can report Coach answers from inside the app.

YOUR DATA
An account is required (email or Google). No ads and no third-party analytics. You can delete your account and
all your data from the app or at https://gym.chrisgoac.dev/delete-account.
Privacy policy (in Spanish): https://gym.chrisgoac.dev/privacy
